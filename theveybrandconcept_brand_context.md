# Veronica Young Brand — Context File

## Brand Identity
- **Name:** Veronica Young Brand
- **Logo:** "VB" cursive monogram inside a thin oval frame; wordmark "VERONICA YOUNG" with "BRAND" in smaller tracked caps beneath
- **Brand colors:** Strictly black, white, and beige (no other colors in brand graphic system)
- **Positioning:** Modern luxury fashion. Elegant, premium, minimalistic but eye-catching, boutique quality. Avoid loud/flashy/discount-store aesthetics.
- **Style references:** Zara, COS, Massimo Dutti, Mango Premium, Saint Laurent campaign aesthetics
- **Product range:** Primarily dresses — evening/dinner gowns, sequin and fringe cocktail dresses. Also two-piece sets and jeans. Earlier merch/lookbook assets also showed sweaters, oxford shirts, water bottles, tote bags, wooden hangers, and business cards.

## Design System
- **Fonts:** Lora (serif — headlines, italic accents) + Poppins (tracked sans — labels, body, CTAs)
- **Color tokens:**
  - Ink (near-black): #17150F
  - Warm white: #FBF9F4
  - Beige: #E7DECB
  - Deep beige: #D6C9AC
  - Beige line/divider: #C7B896
  - Gold accent: #A6875A
- **Signature motif:** thin oval ring echoing the logo's frame, used as a subtle graphic device behind headline text
- Product photography treated with a light, editorial color grade (subtle saturation/contrast boost) — full color, not desaturated

## Campaign History
- **Original brief:** "Mid-Year Sale," 5–10% off all items, in-store + online, 5–10 August
- **Renamed to:** "Summer Sale" (per brand feedback — no longer "Mid-Year")
- **Standing exact-wording requirement for offer copy:**
  "Enjoy 5–10% discount on dresses, dinner gowns, two piece sets and jeans both in-store and on our websites"
- Website links are required on all promotional assets (an earlier draft omitted them; this was corrected)
- Photography can show faces and legs/thighs — earlier assets were deliberately cropped faceless out of caution, but the brand confirmed normal fashion-ad photography (face, legs visible) is fine
- CTA language should be simple and direct: "Shop Now," "Visit Store" — avoid generic template language like "Shop The Edit" or "Visit our website" placeholders
